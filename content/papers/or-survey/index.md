## Why it matters

The survey is a map of how language models enter OR workflows: they can formulate models, write solver code, generate heuristics, or interpret evaluation feedback.

## Core method

This is a literature synthesis rather than a new executable search loop. Its organizing view separates modeling, search, feedback, and deployment concerns across OR tasks.

## Contributions

- A broad organization of LLM roles in operations research.
- Coverage of optimization modeling, search, code generation, and evaluation.
- A boundary map for deciding which OR papers belong in an AHD atlas.

## Strengths and limitations

As a survey, it synthesizes existing evidence rather than introducing a new LLM search loop. Its categories inherit the scope and publication coverage of the review.

## What to improve

Useful follow-ups include stronger behavioral deduplication, cross-task evaluation, and explicit accounting of model and verifier cost.
