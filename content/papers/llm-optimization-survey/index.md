## Why it matters

The overview connects language-model reasoning with optimization modeling, search, evaluation, and human oversight.

## Core method

This survey organizes language-model use in optimization modeling, solver construction, heuristic search, and evaluation. It is included as background for the atlas and should not be read as a new AHD method.

## Contributions

- A conceptual bridge between language models and optimization workflows.
- Discussion of modeling, search, evaluation, and human oversight.
- Terminology for positioning later automatic-design systems.

## Strengths and limitations

This is an overview rather than a new AHD system. Its claims should be read as a snapshot of the field at publication time.

## What to improve

Useful follow-ups include stronger behavioral deduplication, cross-task evaluation, and explicit accounting of model and verifier cost.
